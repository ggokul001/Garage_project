# Attendance & Payroll — Compact Scope

This module is intentionally designed for a small operations team.

## Included

- Shift Master with primary and optional additional shift.
- Daily Attendance grid by date and branch.
- Present, Permission, Half Day Leave, Full Day Leave and Absent statuses.
- Manual entry and Excel-compatible CSV template/import.
- OT hours and permission hours.
- Salary Advance with recovery month.
- Monthly payroll without PF/ESI.
- Salary slip print and monthly salary report export.

## Salary calculation

- Per-day salary = monthly salary / calendar days in the selected month.
- Present and Permission count as one payable day.
- Half Day Leave counts as half a payable day.
- Full Day Leave and Absent have no payable value.
- OT amount = OT hours × employee OT rate.
- Net salary = earned salary + OT + other earnings − salary advance − other deductions.

## Deferred

Biometric device punching is not included now. Attendance keeps a source field so biometric data can be connected later without redesigning payroll.
